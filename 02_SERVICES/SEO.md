# SEO Service

## Service Overview
Strategic search engine optimization that builds organic visibility, drives sustainable traffic growth, and creates a foundation for long-term business success through keyword research, on-page optimization, and content strategy.

## Service Components

### 1. Keyword Research
- Business-relevant keyword identification
- Search intent analysis (informational, navigational, transactional)
- Keyword difficulty assessment
- Search volume analysis
- Keyword clustering and prioritization
- Competitor keyword gap analysis
- Long-tail keyword opportunities
- Local SEO keywords (when applicable)

### 2. On-Page SEO
- Title tag optimization
- Meta description optimization
- Header structure (H1, H2, H3)
- Content optimization for target keywords
- Internal linking strategy
- Image alt text optimization
- URL structure recommendations
- Schema markup recommendations

### 3. Content Strategy
- Content topic planning
- Content calendar development
- Content brief creation
- Blog post strategy
- Landing page content strategy
- Content gap analysis
- Competitor content analysis
- Content refresh strategy for existing pages

### 4. SEO Planning
- Monthly SEO roadmap
- Priority task identification
- Quick-win opportunities
- Long-term strategy development
- Technical SEO audit recommendations
- Competitive positioning analysis
- Seasonal SEO planning

### 5. Local SEO (When Applicable)
- Google Business Profile optimization
- Local citation strategy
- Local keyword targeting
- Review management guidance
- Local content strategy

### 6. Analytics & Reporting
- Organic traffic tracking
- Keyword ranking monitoring
- Click-through rate analysis
- Conversion tracking from organic
- SEO performance summaries
- Progress reporting

## What This Service Delivers
- Clear understanding of keyword opportunities
- Optimized on-page content for search engines
- Strategic content plan for organic growth
- Technical SEO recommendations
- Measurable organic traffic improvement
- Long-term sustainable growth strategy

## Ideal For
- Businesses wanting organic traffic growth
- Ecommerce brands wanting product page optimization
- Content-heavy websites needing SEO strategy
- New websites building SEO foundation
- Businesses wanting to reduce paid ad dependency
- Local businesses wanting local search visibility

## What's NOT Included
- Content writing (strategy and briefs provided, writing separate)
- Technical implementation (recommendations provided)
- Link building / off-page SEO (can be discussed separately)
- Website development
- Social media marketing

## Key Metrics
- Organic traffic growth
- Keyword rankings
- Domain authority
- Organic CTR
- Organic conversions
- Page speed scores
- Core Web Vitals
- Indexed pages
