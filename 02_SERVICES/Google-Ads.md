# Google Ads Service

## Service Overview
Strategic Google advertising campaigns focused on capturing intent-based demand, driving qualified traffic, and generating measurable conversions through search, display, and other Google Ads formats.

## Platforms
- **Google Search Ads** — Intent-based text ads on search results
- **Google Display Network** — Visual ads across websites and apps
- **Google Shopping** — Product listing ads for ecommerce
- **YouTube Ads** — Video advertising on YouTube
- **Performance Max** — AI-powered cross-channel campaigns
- **Google Discovery** — Feed-based ads across Google properties

## Service Components

### 1. Campaign Strategy
- Objective and KPI definition
- Budget planning and allocation
- Campaign type selection
- Keyword strategy development
- Competitive analysis
- Funnel and journey mapping

### 2. Keyword Research
- Search intent analysis
- Keyword clustering and grouping
- Long-tail keyword identification
- Competitor keyword analysis
- Seasonal keyword planning
- Negative keyword strategy

### 3. Ad Copy & Extensions
- Headline and description optimization
- Ad extension strategy (sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets)
- Responsive search ad optimization
- A/B testing frameworks
- Quality score optimization
- Ad relevance improvement

### 4. Campaign Management
- Campaign structure and setup
- Bid strategy optimization
- Budget allocation and pacing
- Ad scheduling optimization
- Geographic targeting
- Device targeting
- Audience layering

### 5. Performance Analysis
- Conversion tracking and analysis
- Cost per conversion monitoring
- Quality score tracking
- Search term analysis
- Impression share analysis
- ROI and ROAS reporting
- Weekly/monthly performance summaries

### 6. Tracking & Measurement
- Google Tag setup guidance
- Conversion action configuration
- Google Analytics 4 integration
- Attribution model selection
- Goal tracking and reporting

## Campaign Types
- **Search Campaigns** — Capture active search intent
- **Display Campaigns** — Brand awareness and remarketing
- **Shopping Campaigns** — Product ads for ecommerce
- **Video Campaigns** — YouTube advertising
- **Performance Max** — AI-driven multi-channel
- **App Campaigns** — Mobile app installs (when applicable)
- **Local Campaigns** — Local business visibility

## What This Service Delivers
- Capture of high-intent search traffic
- Strategic keyword targeting aligned with business goals
- Optimized ad spend with focus on conversions
- Clear performance tracking and reporting
- Continuous improvement through testing
- Scalable paid search systems

## Ideal For
- Businesses wanting to capture search demand
- Service businesses generating leads from search
- Ecommerce brands wanting Shopping campaigns
- B2B companies targeting specific search queries
- Local businesses wanting local visibility
- Brands complementing their Meta Ads strategy

## What's NOT Included
- Website/landing page development
- Creative production (video, display design) — direction only
- SEO (separate service, complementary)
- Content marketing
- Social media management
