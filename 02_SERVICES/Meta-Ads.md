# Meta Ads Service (Facebook & Instagram)

## Service Overview
Performance-focused Meta advertising campaigns designed to drive conversions, increase revenue, and deliver measurable ROI through strategic audience targeting, creative testing, and continuous optimization.

## Platforms
- **Facebook Ads** — Full Facebook advertising ecosystem
- **Instagram Ads** — Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore placements
- **Meta Business Suite** — Campaign management and analytics

## Service Components

### 1. Campaign Strategy
- Objective selection and strategy alignment
- Budget allocation and planning
- Campaign structure design
- Audience research and segmentation
- Funnel mapping (awareness → consideration → conversion)

### 2. Audience Research
- Ideal customer identification
- Custom audience creation strategy
- Lookalike audience development
- Interest-based targeting research
- Competitor audience analysis
- Retargeting strategy design

### 3. Creative Strategy
- Ad creative concepts and direction
- Copy testing frameworks
- Visual creative recommendations
- A/B testing plan for creatives
- UGC-style ad concepts
- Carousel and video ad strategy

### 4. Campaign Management
- Campaign setup and launch
- Daily monitoring and optimization
- Budget pacing and allocation
- Bid strategy optimization
- Placement optimization
- Frequency management

### 5. Performance Analysis
- ROAS and ROI tracking
- Cost per acquisition analysis
- Creative performance breakdown
- Audience performance insights
- Funnel analysis
- Weekly/monthly performance reports
- Recommendations for next steps

### 6. Tracking & Measurement
- Pixel and conversion API setup guidance
- Event tracking strategy
- Attribution setup
- Custom conversion creation
- Analytics integration

## Campaign Types
- **Conversion Campaigns** — Purchase, add to cart, lead generation
- **Traffic Campaigns** — Website visits, landing page traffic
- **Awareness Campaigns** — Brand awareness, reach
- **Retargeting Campaigns** — Cart abandonment, website visitors, engagement audiences
- **Lead Generation** — Lead forms, messenger campaigns
- **Catalog Sales** — Dynamic product ads for ecommerce

## What This Service Delivers
- Strategic ad campaigns aligned with business goals
- Data-driven audience targeting
- Continuous optimization for better performance
- Clear reporting on ad spend and returns
- Creative recommendations based on data
- Scalable advertising systems

## Ideal For
- Ecommerce brands wanting to increase sales
- DTC brands scaling their advertising
- Local businesses driving foot traffic or leads
- Service businesses generating qualified leads
- Brands wanting to scale from organic to paid
- Businesses with existing customer data for retargeting

## What's NOT Included
- Creative production (photography, video, graphic design) — strategy and direction only
- Copywriting (can be separate or client-provided)
- Website/landing page development
- Email marketing
- Influencer partnerships

## Key Metrics
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
- CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)
- CTR (Click-Through Rate)
- CPC (Cost Per Click)
- Conversion rate
- Frequency
- Relevance score
- CPM (Cost Per Mille)
