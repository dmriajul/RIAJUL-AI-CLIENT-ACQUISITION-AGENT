# Social Media Marketing Service

## Service Overview
Strategic social media marketing that builds brand awareness, drives engagement, and supports business growth through consistent, platform-specific content strategies.

## Platforms
- **Instagram** — Visual content, Stories, Reels, Shopping
- **Facebook** — Community building, Page management, Groups
- **TikTok** — Short-form video content, trends, viral content
- **YouTube** — Long-form content, shorts, channel strategy

## Service Components

### 1. Strategy Development
- Platform audit and analysis
- Competitor research
- Target audience identification
- Content pillars development
- Growth strategy roadmap

### 2. Content Strategy
- Content calendar creation
- Content mix planning (educational, entertaining, promotional)
- Visual style guide development
- Caption and copy frameworks
- Hashtag strategy

### 3. Content Creation Direction
- Carousel post strategy
- Short-form video concepts (Reels, TikTok)
- Story content planning
- Static post design direction
- User-generated content strategy

### 4. Platform Management
- Posting schedule optimization
- Engagement management
- Community building
- Comment and DM handling strategy
- Platform-specific best practices

### 5. Growth & Engagement
- Follower growth strategies
- Engagement rate optimization
- Collaboration and partnership strategy
- Trend-based content opportunities
- Audience interaction techniques

## What This Service Delivers
- Consistent brand presence across platforms
- Strategic content that serves business goals
- Increased engagement and community interaction
- Better understanding of target audience
- Foundation for paid advertising campaigns

## Ideal For
- Ecommerce brands wanting stronger social presence
- DTC brands needing content strategy
- Startups building brand awareness
- Small businesses wanting professional social media
- Coaches and consultants building personal brands

## Service Does NOT Include
- Full content production (photography, video editing) — direction and planning only
- Paid advertising (separate service)
- Influencer management (can be added as scope)
- Website development
- Graphic design (content direction provided, design by client or separate designer)

## Key Metrics
- Follower growth rate
- Engagement rate
- Reach and impressions
- Content performance by type
- Audience demographics
- Website traffic from social (when tracked)
