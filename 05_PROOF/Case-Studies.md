## Case Study #6: Medical Clinic — Local SEO & ORM (Australia)

**Client:** Premier Medical & Aesthetic Clinic (anonymized)
**Industry:** Healthcare / Local Clinic
**Country:** Australia
**Service:** Local SEO & Online Reputation Management

### The Situation
A premium localized clinic was stuck on page 2 of Google Maps, invisible to high-value local patients searching for aesthetic and medical services.

### The Challenge
- Dominate Google Maps 3-Pack across a 15km suburban radius
- Build authentic 5-star review volume
- Increase inbound phone calls and direction requests

### The Strategy
- GBP audit and complete restructuring
- Local Falcon geo-grid rank tracking
- Automated SMS review funnel after patient visits
- NAP consistency across AU directories (TrueLocal, YellowPages AU, HiPages)
- Weekly geo-tagged GBP posts and media uploads

### The Results
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Maps 3-Pack Rank | #1 to #3 (across 15km radius) |
| 5-Star Reviews Added | +74 (from 11 to 85+) |
| Inbound Phone Calls | +148% |
| Direction Requests | +92% |

### When to Use This Case Study
- Local businesses in Australia or UAE
- Healthcare clinics and professional practices
- Businesses needing Google Maps visibility
- Businesses needing review generation
- Any "near me" search dominated industry
