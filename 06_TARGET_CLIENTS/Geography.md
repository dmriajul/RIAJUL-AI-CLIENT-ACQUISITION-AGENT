## Geographic Research Notes

When researching a prospect, always note:
1. **Where are they based?** (Country, city)
2. **Where do they operate?** (Local, national, global)
3. **Where are their customers?** (Their target market)
4. **What's their time zone?** (For outreach scheduling)
5. **What service fits their geography?** (Meta Ads for US/UK, Local SEO for AU/UAE)
