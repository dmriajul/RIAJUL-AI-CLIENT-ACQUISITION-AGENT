# Outreach Rules

## The Golden Rule

> **Don't try to sell to everyone. Find businesses where Riajul's skills genuinely match a visible opportunity. Research before outreach. Personalize based on evidence. Never invent proof. Prioritize relevance over volume.**

---

## Outreach DO's

### ✅ DO:
- **Be specific** — Reference something real about their business
- **Be brief** — Cold emails under 150 words, LinkedIn DMs even shorter
- **Be human** — Write like a person, not a template
- **Be relevant** — Connect your observation to their business
- **Be helpful** — Offer value before asking for anything
- **Be honest** — Only claim what you can prove
- **Be patient** — Give them time to respond
- **Be respectful** — Accept "no" gracefully
- **Be consistent** — Follow the follow-up schedule
- **Be contextual** — Match the channel's tone (email vs. LinkedIn)
- **Be conversational** — Ask questions, don't just broadcast
- **Be prepared** — Have relevant portfolio/proof ready if they respond

### ✅ Every outreach message must have:
1. A specific reference to their business (something you actually observed)
2. A clear connection between your observation and their potential need
3. A soft, low-pressure call to action
4. An easy way to opt out

---

## Outreach DON'Ts

### ❌ DON'T:
- **Fake personalization** — "I love your content" when you haven't seen it
- **Fake results** — "I helped a brand get 1000% ROI"
- **Fake clients** — Inventing client names or testimonials
- **Fake case studies** — Creating fake success stories
- **Exaggerate** — "I'm the best Meta Ads specialist in the world"
- **Long sales pitches** — Don't write novels in cold outreach
- **Spammy urgency** — "Only 2 spots left!", "Offer expires today!"
- **"Guaranteed results"** — "I guarantee 5x ROAS"
- **Generic templates** — Same message to everyone
- **Batch sending** — Personalize every single message
- **Follow up too much** — Max 3 follow-ups total
- **Guilt-trip** — "You haven't responded to my emails"
- **Be pushy** — "You NEED this", "Your business will fail without..."
- **Be vague** — "I can help grow your business" (how?)
- **Oversell** — More promises than you can deliver
- **Contact outside business hours** — Respect their timezone
- **Use multiple channels simultaneously** — Space out email and LinkedIn
- **Pitch in connection requests** — Connect first, pitch later

## Message Quality Checklist

Before sending ANY outreach message, verify:

### For Cold Emails:
- [ ] Under 150 words?
- [ ] Specific reference to their business?
- [ ] Clear connection to potential need?
- [ ] Soft, low-pressure CTA?
- [ ] No exaggeration or false claims?
- [ ] No fake urgency?
- [ ] Human approval received?
- [ ] Relevant proof ready if they respond?

### For LinkedIn Messages:
- [ ] Conversational tone?
- [ ] Under 100 words?
- [ ] Not pitching in first message?
- [ ] Specific observation included?
- [ ] Question or invitation to respond?
- [ ] No template feel?
- [ ] Human approval received?

### For Follow-Ups:
- [ ] New value added (not just "checking in")?
- [ ] Shorter than the original message?
- [ ] Appropriate time gap since last message?
- [ ] Still relevant to their business?
- [ ] Not more than 3 follow-ups total?
- [ ] Human approval received?

## Channel Rules

### Email
- Max 4 messages total (initial + 3 follow-ups)
- Wait 3-5 days between follow-ups
- Always reply in same thread
- Subject lines: short, specific, not salesy
- Always get human approval before sending

### LinkedIn
- Max 3 messages total (connection + 2 follow-ups)
- Wait 5-7 days between follow-ups
- Don't pitch in connection request
- Keep messages very short
- Engage with their content first when possible
- Always get human approval before sending

### Cross-Channel
- Don't email AND LinkedIn on the same day
- Space cross-channel outreach by 3+ days
- If they don't respond on one channel, try the other after 5+ days
- Max total touchpoints: 6 (across all channels)

## Personalization Levels

### Level 1 — Basic (Minimum Acceptable)
- Company name
- Industry
- Website

### Level 2 — Relevant (Standard)
- Specific observation about their business
- Platform-specific insight (their Instagram, their ads, their website)
- Connection to potential need

### Level 3 — Deep (Ideal — when evidence supports it)
- Specific observation with evidence
- Business opportunity identified
- Relevant service recommendation
- Relevant proof/case study
- Natural conversation angle

**Default: Level 3** when sufficient research is available.
**Minimum: Level 2** for any outreach.
**Never acceptable: Level 1 only.**

## Anti-Spam Rules

1. **Never send more than 20 cold emails per day** (when system is mature)
2. **Never send more than 10 LinkedIn connection requests per day**
3. **Always personalize** — no batch sending
4. **Space out sends** — don't send 20 emails in 5 minutes
5. **Respect opt-outs** — immediately remove anyone who says stop
6. **Don't contact the same person on multiple channels simultaneously**
7. **Keep clean records** — track everything in CRM
8. **Follow CAN-SPAM guidelines** (for US prospects):
   - Include your name/business
   - Provide way to opt out
   - Don't use misleading subject lines
   - Include physical address (when required)

---

## When NOT to Reach Out

Do not send outreach if:
1. ❌ No specific observation about their business
2. ❌ No clear opportunity identified
3. ❌ No relevant service match
4. ❌ No relevant proof available
5. ❌ They've explicitly said "not interested"
6. ❌ They've asked to be removed
7. ❌ You can't personalize beyond company name
8. ❌ They're clearly not in your target market
9. ❌ You haven't gotten human approval
10. ❌ You're reaching out just to hit a number

**Quality > Quantity. Always.**
