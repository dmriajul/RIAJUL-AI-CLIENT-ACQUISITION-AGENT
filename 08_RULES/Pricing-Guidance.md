## How to Handle Pricing Questions

### When prospect asks "How much do you charge?"

**Step 1:** Acknowledge the question
**Step 2:** Ask about their scope/goals (to determine the right tier)
**Step 3:** Share the relevant range
**Step 4:** Suggest a call for specifics

### Response Template:

```
Good question — it depends on scope and what makes most sense for [Company].

For [specific service], my clients typically invest between $[X]–$[Y] per month, which includes [brief scope description].

But the exact number depends on your goals, current situation, and what channels make most sense.

Happy to discuss what would work best. Would a quick 15-min call make sense?
[Calendly link]
```

### When to share pricing vs. suggest call:

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| They directly ask "how much" | Share the range for the relevant service |
| They ask for a proposal | Gather requirements first, then suggest call |
| Early in conversation | Don't lead with price — understand needs first |
| They compare with others | Share range + highlight what's included |
