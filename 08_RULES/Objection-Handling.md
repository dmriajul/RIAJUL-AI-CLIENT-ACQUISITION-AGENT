# Objection Handling Guide

> Structured responses for common prospect objections.
> All responses follow brand voice: professional, warm, honest, low-pressure.

---

## Objection #1: "We already have someone handling our marketing"

**What it means:** They have a marketing provider (freelancer, agency, or in-house). Not necessarily happy with them.

**Response Strategy:** Don't attack their current provider. Position as a complement or alternative for when they're ready.

**Response Template:**
```
Totally understand — glad you have someone covering that.

I'm not looking to replace anyone. A lot of the clients I work with initially reached out just to have a second perspective on their [Meta Ads / social strategy / tracking setup].

If you ever want a free audit of your current [ads / social media / tracking] — no obligation — I'm happy to share what I'd do differently. Completely up to you.

Either way, keep up the great work with [Company].
```

**Key Points:**
- Respect their current arrangement
- Offer free audit as value (not a pitch)
- Leave door open
- Don't be pushy

## Objection #2: "We don't have budget for this right now"

**What it means:** Budget is tight or allocated elsewhere.

**Response Strategy:** Acknowledge, provide value, stay in touch.

**Response Template:**

```
Happy to share a few ideas whenever it makes sense. No rush at all.

Wishing [Company] continued growth!
```

## Objection #3: "We tried ads before and they didn't work"

**What it means:** Bad past experience. Distrustful of marketing services.

**Response Strategy:** Acknowledge the frustration. Differentiate your approach (tracking, data-driven, transparent).

**Response Template:**
```
That's frustrating — and honestly more common than people think. A lot of businesses have been burned by ads that were set up without proper tracking or strategy.

The approach I take is a bit different. Before spending a dollar on ads, I set up server-side tracking (Meta CAPI + GA4) so we can actually see what's working. Then we start with small test budgets and only scale what's proven.

I'd be happy to take a look at your previous ad account and share what I think went wrong — even if you don't end up working with me. No strings attached.

Would that be helpful?
```

## Objection Handling Rules

### DO:
- ✅ Acknowledge their concern first
- ✅ Be honest and transparent
- ✅ Provide value even in objection responses
- ✅ Leave the door open
- ✅ Match the specific objection with the right response
- ✅ Never be defensive or argumentative
- ✅ Keep responses short and human

### DON'T:
- ❌ Attack their current provider
- ❌ Promise guaranteed results
- ❌ Be pushy or high-pressure
- ❌ Make them feel bad for objecting
- ❌ Use fake urgency ("This offer expires...")
- ❌ Ignore their concern and keep pitching
- ❌ Send long essays as responses
