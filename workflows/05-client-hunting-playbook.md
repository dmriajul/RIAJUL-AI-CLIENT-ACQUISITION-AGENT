## 7. Buying Signals

### 🔴 High Intent (Contact Immediately)
- Running ads with obvious issues (poor creative, high frequency)
- Posted about marketing challenges or needs
- Just launched new product/collection
- Actively hiring for marketing roles
- Meta Pixel installed but Ad Library empty
- Competitors running ads but they're not

### 🟡 Medium Intent (Research More, Then Contact)
- Active social media but inconsistent
- Good product but weak marketing
- Recently rebranded or redesigned website
- Growing team (indicates scaling)
- Low Google review count (for local)
- Not in Maps 3-Pack (for local AU/UAE)

### ⚪ Low Intent (Skip for Now)
- No website or social presence
- Already using large agency (high switching cost)
- No budget signals at all
- Very early stage with no product
- Outside target geography

## 13. Qualification Rules (A/B/C)

### A — Strong Fit (Contact immediately)
- ✅ Industry matches verified experience
- ✅ Clear marketing opportunity visible (evidence-based)
- ✅ Decision maker accessible
- ✅ Budget signals present (running ads, hiring, growing)
- ✅ In target geography
- ✅ Relevant case study available

### B — Potential Fit (Contact with caution)
- ✅ Some alignment but gaps exist
- ✅ Opportunity exists but less clear
- ⚠️ May need more research
- ⚠️ May not have exact case study match

### C — Weak Fit (Do NOT contact)
- ❌ Significant misalignment
- ❌ No clear opportunity visible
- ❌ Wrong geography
- ❌ No budget signals
- ❌ No relevant proof available

**Minimum for outreach: B+ with evidence-based observation**

---

## 14. Disqualification Rules

Do NOT contact if ANY of these are true:
- No website or online presence
- Outside target geography (unless explicitly targeting remote)
- No visible marketing activity at all
- Company size > 100 employees (too large)
- Already using a large agency (high switching cost)
- No decision maker accessible
- Industry is heavily regulated (pharma, finance, legal) unless specifically positioned
- No product/service to market (idea stage only)

---

## 15. Personalization Rules

### Level 1 — Basic (Minimum, NEVER use alone)
- Company name + industry

### Level 2 — Relevant (Minimum acceptable)
- Specific observation about their business (from research)
- Connection to potential marketing opportunity

### Level 3 — Deep (Default target)
- Specific observation WITH evidence
- Identified business opportunity
- Relevant service recommendation
- Relevant proof (case study/portfolio)
- Natural conversation angle

**Rule:** If you cannot achieve Level 2, do not send outreach.
