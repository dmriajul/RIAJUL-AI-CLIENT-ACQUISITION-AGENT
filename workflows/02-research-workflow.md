# Research Workflow

```
Find clients
Research [company name]
Research prospects in [industry]

```

---

## Research Process

### Phase 1: Discovery
**Goal:** Find potential prospects matching ICP criteria.

**Sources:**
- LinkedIn company search
- Website/brand discovery
- Industry directories
- Competitor client analysis
- Social media exploration (Instagram, Facebook)
- Meta Ad Library (companies running ads)
- Google searches by industry/location

**What to look for:**
- Companies matching target industries
- Active online presence (website, social media)
- Decision makers accessible
- Geographic fit (USA primary, UK/CA/AU secondary)
- Company size fit (1-50 employees ideal)

---

### Phase 2: Deep Research
**Goal:** Gather comprehensive information about each prospect.

**Research Checklist:**

#### Company Information
- [ ] Company name
- [ ] Website URL
- [ ] Industry/category
- [ ] Country/location
- [ ] Company size (employee count from LinkedIn)
- [ ] Founded year (if available)
- [ ] Products/services offered
- [ ] Target audience/customers

#### Online Presence
- [ ] Website quality and functionality
- [ ] Blog/content presence
- [ ] Instagram presence and activity
- [ ] Facebook presence and activity
- [ ] LinkedIn presence
- [ ] Other social platforms

#### Marketing Activity
- [ ] Social media posting frequency
- [ ] Content quality and consistency
- [ ] Engagement levels
- [ ] Ad Library (Meta) — running ads?
- [ ] Google Ads presence (if visible)
- [ ] Email marketing (if visible)
- [ ] SEO indicators (blog, keywords)

#### Decision Maker
- [ ] Name
- [ ] Role/title
- [ ] LinkedIn profile URL
- [ ] Public email (if available)
- [ ] Communication style (from LinkedIn posts)

#### Opportunity Signals
- [ ] Marketing gaps identified
- [ ] Competitor comparison (if applicable)
- [ ] Growth indicators (hiring, new products, etc.)
- [ ] Recent changes (rebrand, new launch, etc.)
- [ ] Pain points (from public content, reviews, etc.)

---

### Phase 3: Opportunity Detection
**Goal:** Identify specific opportunities where Riajul can add value.

**For each prospect, answer:**

1. **What is happening?**
   - What marketing activities are they currently doing?
   - What's working? What's not?
   - What patterns do you see?

2. **What opportunity exists?**
   - Where are the gaps?
   - What could be improved?
   - What's missing from their marketing?

3. **Which service solves it?**
   - Meta Ads (if they need paid social)
   - Google Ads (if they need search visibility)
   - SEO (if they need organic growth)
   - SMM (if they need social strategy)
   - Combination of services

4. **Why is Riajul relevant?**
   - Matching experience in their industry
   - Relevant service expertise
   - Similar client results
   - Specific skills that address their gap

---

### Phase 4: Qualification
**Goal:** Assess prospect fit and assign priority.

**A — Strong Fit:**
- ✅ Industry matches expertise
- ✅ Clear marketing opportunity visible
- ✅ Decision maker accessible
- ✅ Budget signals present
- ✅ Service need aligns with available services
- ✅ Geographic match

**B — Potential Fit:**
- ✅ Some alignment but gaps exist
- ✅ Opportunity exists but less clear
- ✅ May need more research
- ✅ Worth pursuing with personalized approach

**C — Weak Fit:**
- ❌ Significant misalignment
- ❌ No clear opportunity visible
- ❌ Wrong industry, size, or geography
- ❌ Do not pursue

## Research Quality Rules

1. **Only use publicly available information**
2. **Distinguish between facts and observations**
3. **Label assumptions clearly**
4. **Verify from multiple sources when possible**
5. **Note when information is uncertain**
6. **Never access private/restricted information**
7. **Never fabricate company details**
8. **Be specific, not vague**
