# Email Outreach Frameworks

## Core Principles
- Short (under 150 words for cold email)
- Human (not templated-sounding)
- Specific (reference something real about them)
- Relevant (connect to their business, not your services)
- Low-pressure (no urgency, no hard sell)
- Conversation-focused (ask a question, don't pitch)

---

## Framework 1: Observation → Opportunity → Soft Ask

**Structure:**
```
Subject: [Brief, relevant — NOT salesy]

Hi [Name],

[Specific observation about their business — something you actually noticed]

[Connect observation to a potential opportunity or gap]

[Soft, low-pressure question or offer]

[Name]
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: Quick thought on [Brand]'s Instagram

Hi [Name],

I came across [Brand] on Instagram — love what you're doing with [specific product/collection].

I noticed you're posting consistently but haven't started using Reels much. In the [industry] space, Reels are driving significant reach right now.

I help brands build content strategies that actually drive results — happy to share a few ideas if you're interested.

No pressure either way.

Riajul
```

## Framework 2: Research → Value → Question

**Structure:**
```
Subject: [Relevant hook]

Hi [Name],

I was researching [industry/niche] brands and came across [Brand].

[Share a specific insight from your research — what you noticed, what you think about their marketing]

[Offer a specific piece of value — idea, observation, resource]

[Ask a genuine question]

Riajul
```

---

## Framework 3: Problem → Relevant Experience → Question

**Structure:**
```
Subject: [Problem-relevant subject]

Hi [Name],

[Reference a common challenge in their industry]

I've worked with [similar type of brand] on [relevant service], and [brief result or insight].

[Ask if they've experienced this challenge or if they're handling it differently]

Riajul
```

## Follow-Up Frameworks

### Follow-Up #1 (3-5 days after initial email)

**Structure:**
```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

Just following up on my last email. I know things get busy.

[Add one new piece of value or a different angle]

Happy to chat briefly if it's relevant — no pressure.

Riajul
```

### Follow-Up #2 (5-7 days after Follow-Up #1)

**Structure:**
```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

Last note from me on this.

[Brief, one-line value add or new angle]

If it's not the right time, completely understand. Happy to stay in touch.

Riajul
```

### Follow-Up #3 — Breakup (7-10 days after Follow-Up #2)

**Structure:**
```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

I haven't heard back and I don't want to keep filling your inbox.

If marketing support ever becomes a priority, feel free to reach out. Wishing [Brand] continued growth.

Riajul
```

## Subject Line Rules

### DO:
- Keep it short (under 6 words)
- Be specific and relevant
- Reference their brand or something specific
- Sound like a human wrote it
- Create mild curiosity

### DON'T:
- Use all caps
- Use exclamation marks
- Sound salesy ("Amazing opportunity!", "Grow your business!")
- Use generic templates ("Quick question", "Following up")
- Be misleading or clickbait

### Good Examples:
- "Quick thought on [Brand]'s ads"
- "[Brand] + Instagram strategy"
- "Noticed something on your website"
- "Idea for [Brand]"
- "Your Instagram content"

### Bad Examples:
- "GROW YOUR BUSINESS 10X!"
- "Quick Question About Your Marketing"
- "Partnership Opportunity"
- "I Can Help You"
- "Marketing Services"

---

## Email Rules

1. **Never send more than 3 follow-ups** (initial + 3 follow-ups = 4 total)
2. **Wait at least 3 days between follow-ups**
3. **Always reply to the original thread** (don't start new emails)
4. **Never fake urgency** ("Limited spots", "Only this week")
5. **Never guarantee results** ("I guarantee 5x ROAS")
6. **Always make it easy to say no** ("No pressure either way")
7. **Keep each email shorter than the last**
8. **Personalize every email** — no batch-sending without personalization
9. **Human approval required** before sending any cold email
