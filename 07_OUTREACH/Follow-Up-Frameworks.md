# Follow-Up Frameworks

## Follow-Up Philosophy

Follow-ups are NOT about being annoying. They are about:
- Showing persistence (in a good way)
- Providing additional value each time
- Being top-of-mind when timing is right
- Giving them multiple chances to respond

Most deals are closed after follow-up #3 or #4, not the first message.

---

## Follow-Up Timing

### Email Follow-Up Schedule
| Follow-Up | Timing | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Initial Email | Day 0 | First contact |
| Follow-Up #1 | Day 3-5 | Gentle nudge + new value |
| Follow-Up #2 | Day 8-10 | Different angle + value |
| Follow-Up #3 (Breakup) | Day 15-17 | Final attempt, graceful exit |

### LinkedIn Follow-Up Schedule
| Follow-Up | Timing | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Connection Request | Day 0 | Connect |
| First DM | After acceptance | Introduction |
| Follow-Up #1 | Day 5-7 | Gentle nudge |
| Follow-Up #2 (Breakup) | Day 12-14 | Final attempt |

## Follow-Up Email Templates

### Follow-Up #1: The Value Add

**Goal:** Add value, don't just "check in"

```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

Following up on my note from last week.

Since then, I [noticed something new / had an idea / found something relevant]:

[One specific piece of value — observation, idea, insight about their business]

Happy to share more if relevant. No pressure either way.

Riajul
```

### Follow-Up #2: The Different Angle

**Goal:** Try a different approach than the first email

```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

I realize I might have caught you at a bad time.

Quick thought from a different angle: [Share a different observation, different service angle, or different pain point]

If it's not relevant right now, completely understand.

Riajul
```

### Follow-Up #3: The Breakup

**Goal:** Graceful exit, leave door open

```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

I don't want to keep filling your inbox, so this will be my last note.

If marketing support becomes a priority down the line, feel free to reach out. Happy to help whenever the timing makes sense.

Wishing [Brand] continued growth!

Best,
Riajul
```

## Follow-Up Value Angles

Each follow-up should bring NEW value. Rotate through these angles:

### Angle 1: New Observation
"I noticed [something new about their business]..."

### Angle 2: Industry Insight
"Been seeing a trend in [industry] where [observation]..."

### Angle 3: Relevant Content
"Came across this [article/resource] that made me think of [Company]..."

### Angle 4: Specific Idea
"Had a quick idea for [specific thing] — [brief idea]..."

### Angle 5: Different Service Angle
"Also wanted to mention — I also help with [different service], which might be relevant because..."

### Angle 6: Social Proof
"Recently helped a [similar type of brand] with [specific result]..."

## Follow-Up Rules

### DO:
- ✅ Always reply to the original thread (email)
- ✅ Bring new value in each follow-up
- ✅ Keep follow-ups shorter than the initial message
- ✅ Space follow-ups appropriately
- ✅ Make it easy to opt out
- ✅ Max 3 follow-ups total

### DON'T:
- ❌ Send "Just following up" with no new value
- ❌ Follow up daily
- ❌ Follow up more than 3 times
- ❌ Guilt-trip ("You haven't responded...")
- ❌ Fake urgency ("Last chance", "Only this week")
- ❌ Change subject lines (keep thread)
- ❌ Follow up if they've explicitly said no

## Follow-Up Tracking

When tracking follow-ups in CRM, always note:
1. **Date sent**
2. **Channel** (Email / LinkedIn)
3. **Angle used** (What value was provided)
4. **Response received** (Yes/No — and what)
5. **Next action** (Wait / Follow up / Close / Re-engage)
6. **Next follow-up date** (if applicable)
