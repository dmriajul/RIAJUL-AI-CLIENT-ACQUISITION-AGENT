# LinkedIn Outreach Frameworks

## Core Principles
- Conversational, not salesy
- Short messages (LinkedIn DMs should be very brief)
- Build connection before pitching
- Reference something specific about them
- One message, one purpose
- Respect their time and inbox

---

## Connection Request Messages

### Framework 1: Shared Interest/Industry

```
Hi [Name], I came across your profile while researching [industry] brands. Love what you're building at [Company]. Would love to connect.
```

### Framework 2: Content Engagement

```
Hi [Name], I've been following your content about [topic]. Really enjoyed your post about [specific post]. Would love to connect.
```

### Framework 3: Mutual Connection/Group

```
Hi [Name], noticed we're both connected to [person/group]. Your work at [Company] caught my attention. Would love to connect.
```

### Rules for Connection Requests:
- Keep under 300 characters (LinkedIn limit)
- Don't pitch in the connection request
- Mention something specific
- Be genuine

## First DM (After Connection Accepted)

### Framework 1: Casual Introduction

```
Hey [Name], thanks for connecting!

I checked out [Company] — really like [specific thing]. 

What's your biggest marketing challenge right now? Always curious to learn what [industry] brands are dealing with.
```

### Framework 2: Value-First

```
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!

I noticed [specific observation about their marketing/business].

I work with [type of brand] on [relevant area] — happy to share a quick thought on [specific thing] if helpful. No pitch, just an idea.
```

### Framework 3: Research-Based

```
Hi [Name], great to connect.

I was looking at [Company]'s [social media/website/ads] and noticed [specific observation].

Not sure if this is relevant for you right now, but [brief insight or idea].

Would love to hear your thoughts.
```

## Follow-Up DMs

### Follow-Up #1 (After no response to first DM — 5-7 days)

```
Hey [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried.

[Brief new angle or value add — one sentence]

No rush, just thought it might be relevant.
```

### Follow-Up #2 (After no response to Follow-Up #1 — 7-10 days)

```
Hi [Name], I don't want to keep messaging if the timing isn't right.

If [marketing/ads/social media] ever becomes a priority, happy to chat. Otherwise, love following your content — keep it up!
```

## Conversation Reply Frameworks

### When They Show Interest

```
Great to hear! 

[Answer their question or respond to their comment]

Would it make sense to have a quick chat about this? Happy to share some ideas based on what I've seen.

[Optional: suggest a time or ask for their availability]
```

### When They Ask About Pricing

```
Good question — pricing depends on the scope and what makes most sense for [Company].

For [service type], my clients typically invest between [range] per month. But it really depends on [specific factors].

Happy to discuss what would work best for your situation. Would a quick call make sense?
```

### When They Ask About Experience

```
Sure — I've been working on [relevant area] for [time period].

Most recently, I worked with [type of brand] on [relevant project]. [Brief result — only if real and specific].

You can also check my LinkedIn profile for more details: [URL]

Happy to go deeper on anything specific.
```

### When They Say "Not Right Now"

```
Totally understand, [Name]. Thanks for being upfront.

I'll keep following [Company]'s journey — looks like great things ahead. 

Feel free to reach out anytime if things change.
```

### When They're Not Interested

```
No problem at all, [Name]. Appreciate you letting me know.

Keep up the great work with [Company] — I'll be cheering from the sidelines!
```

## LinkedIn-Specific Rules

### DO:
- ✅ Engage with their content before sending a connection request (like, comment)
- ✅ Keep messages conversational and short
- ✅ Use their name naturally
- ✅ Ask questions that invite response
- ✅ Accept that not everyone will respond
- ✅ Move slowly — LinkedIn is a relationship platform

### DON'T:
- ❌ Send a pitch in the first message
- ❌ Send voice notes to strangers
- ❌ Send long paragraphs
- ❌ Use "Dear" or overly formal language
- ❌ Send the same message to everyone
- ❌ Follow up more than 2 times on LinkedIn
- ❌ Connect with the sole purpose of immediately selling
- ❌ Send connection requests without a note (when possible)
